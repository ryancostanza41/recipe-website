Miami Rice (and Sausage Skillet)

Crockett and Tubbs are eating in style with this savory dish, filled with hearty sausage and rice, mixed with vegetables in a fire-roasted tomato base

~ Ingredients ~

Seasoning Blend

  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, or italian herb blend
  • ¼ tsp ground thyme
  • ¼ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 dash cayenne pepper (see note 1)

Everything Else

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 14oz—18oz sausage of your choice, sliced (see note 2)
  • ⅓ cup white wine (see note 3)
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • ½ red onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp butter, unsalted
  • 15oz can fire roasted diced tomatoes, do not drain
  • 1½ cups chicken broth
  • 1 cup white rice
  • 15.5oz can black beans, drained and rinsed

~ Instructions ~

  1. Combine seasoning blend into a small cup and mix well.
  2. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. Once the skillet is heated, add the sausage into the oil. Allow it to brown well on both sides, stirring occassionally. About 10—15 minutes, then remove sausage from skillet, set aside, and lower temperature to medium heat.
  4. Pour white wine into the still-hot skillet. Use a rubber spatula to scrape the bottom of the pan. Allow the wine to reduce by half, about 2—3 minutes.
  5. Add the green bell pepper, red onion, and garlic into the skillet and allow to cook for about 10 minutes, stirring occassionally.
  6. add the butter into the pan, along with half of the seasoning blend (roughly ½ tbsp) and mix well. Once the butter is fully melted, remove the vegetables from the skillet and set aside.
  7. add the can of fire roasted tomatoes, along with the juice in the can, into the pan. Use a rubber spatula to scrape the bottom of the pan. Add chicken broth.
  8. Once the broth begins to boil, add rice and the remaining seasoning blend to the skillet, lower the temperature to low heat, and cook covered for 15 minutes.
  9. Add the black beans, sausage and vegetables back into the pan and stir well. Continue to cook covered until the liquid is cooked off and sausage and veggies are thoroughly heated, roughly 5 more minutes.
  10. Remove from heat, and enjoy!

~ Notes ~

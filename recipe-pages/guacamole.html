Holy Guacamole, Batman!

After a tough night of fighting crime and mourning the death of his parents, the Dark Knight takes a dark bite out of this tasty guac - giving him the strength to fight another day!

Tools & Equipment

  • Bowl with airtight lid: to mix the ingredients, and store in fridge.

  • Measuring cups & spoons: to measure out all ingredients.

  • Kitchen knife: to cut the avocados and limes in half, chop the cilantro, and dice the onion.

  • Lime juicer: do I really need to explain the purpose of a lime juicer?

  • Spoon: to scoop the flesh out of the avocados.

  • Fork: to mash the avocados to your desired consistency, and mix all ingredients.

~ Ingredients ~

  • 6 ripe avocados
  • ¼ cup lime juice, freshly squeezed (about 2-3 limes)
  • ¼ cup cilantro, densely packed & lightly chopped
  • ½ cup red onion, diced (about ½ of a large red onion)
  • ½ tsp chili powder
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp ground black pepper

~ Instructions ~

  1. Cut the avocados in half, separate the halves and remove the pits. Use a spoon to remove the tasty innards. Place innards in a medium bowl.
  2. Using a fork or your hands (with gloves on), mash up the avocados to desired consistency.
  3. Add all remaining ingredients (cilantro, red onion, chili powder, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper) to the avocados and mix until well incorporated.
  4. Move to an airtight container and store in the refrigerator for at least two hours, or until chilled.
  5. Enjoy with tortilla chips, veggies, or just eat it with a spoon. I won't tell anyone!

~ Notes ~

  • For a spicy guacamole, add 1 diced jalapeño (seeds removed) to the recipe, or alternatively you can add ¼ tsp cayenne pepper.
  • Some people like tomato in their guac, so feel free to add 1-2 diced plum tomatoes to the recipe. I don't, but you can. I really don't care.
  • Some people don't like red onion. If you're one of those losers, you can use a ½ cup of yellow onion instead. Or a sweet onion. Or no onion at all. Again, I really don't care what you do.
  • If you don't know how to tell if an avocado is ripe, ask google.
